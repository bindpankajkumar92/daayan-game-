## Shots

### 1. Sunsaan Raasta · 5s
Dhund bhari raat, khaali raasta, akela aadmi bike pe.
```
Wide establishing shot at night. A quiet rural road winds past ordinary leafy trees and low hedges under a huge full moon, with a light natural mist and a few distant village windows glowing warm. A single headlight approaches: the young Indian man (olive-green jacket, red muffler, jeans, open-face helmet) rides an old black motorcycle past the camera, leaning slightly into the curve, his red muffler whipping in the wind. Side-tracking camera follows the bike, then lets it ride away down the road. Soft blue moonlight, calm village-edge atmosphere - normal and grounded, not a nightmare forest. The headlight is the only strong warm light.
```
Audio: engine ki awaaz, jhingur, hawa.

### 2. Bike Band · 7s
Headlight tim-timati hai, engine band. Kick maarta hai, kuch nahi. Dhund me door ek akela ghar dikhta hai.
```
Medium shot on the empty foggy road. The motorcycle's headlight flickers and dies, the engine coughs and stops, and the bike rolls to a halt. The young Indian man (olive-green jacket, red muffler, jeans) pulls off his helmet and kicks the starter twice, nothing. He looks around nervously. The camera slowly pans past him to reveal, far away in the fog, an old lonely two-storey house with one warm glowing window.
```
Audio: engine khaansta hai → sannata, ullu. AADMI (dheere se): "Oh, come on... not now."

### 3. Darwaza · 8s
Wo knock karne hi wala hai ki darwaza khud khul jaata hai. Andar lalten liye lambi budhi aurat, meethi muskaan.
```
Low-angle shot on the wooden porch of the old house: cracked plaster walls, two wooden posts, a heavy old wooden front door. The young Indian man (olive-green jacket, red muffler, jeans) lifts his hand to knock, but the door creaks open by itself. Inside stands the tall gaunt woman (weathered face, dark auburn curly hair, cream and orange check cardigan with brown trim, dark jeans, barefoot), a head taller than him, holding an old metal lantern and smiling warmly. Warm lantern light spills out into the blue fog.
```
AADMI: "Ma'am... my bike broke down. I just want to go home."
LADY: "Where would you go at this hour, dear? Stay here tonight."

### 4. Teen Taale · 3s
Wo andar jaata hai. Peeche lady chupchaap darwaze pe teen taale laga deti hai.
```
Close-up on the inside of the heavy wooden front door. An old wrinkled hand snaps three old iron padlocks shut, one after another. In the soft-focus background the young man (red muffler) walks further into the dark hall, unaware. Warm lantern light, deep shadows.
```
Audio: click... click... CLICK.

### 5. Lalten · 4s
Chhota store room: purana bed, bada almirah. Lady lalten pakda ke andhere me chali jaati hai.
```
A small cluttered store room: an old single wooden bed in the corner, a tall old wooden wardrobe with two doors, dusty boxes, a tiny moonlit window. The tall gaunt woman (cream and orange check cardigan, dark jeans) hands the old metal lantern to the young man (red muffler), smiles, and walks out into the dark. He sits down on the bed.
```
LADY: "There's no power... keep this with you."

### 6. Aadhi Raat · 4s
Wo so raha hai. Neeche se dheemi, fusfusati chanting. Aankhen khulti hain.
```
Night in the small store room, moonlight through the tiny window. The young man (red muffler) sleeps on the old bed, the lantern turned low beside him. His eyes snap open. He sits up slowly, listening, staring down at the floor in fear.
```
Audio: neeche se dheemi, samajh na aane wali chanting (koi asli mantra nahi), ghar ki charr-charr.

### 7. Parde Pe Parchhai · 5s
Lalten leke basement me utarta hai. Door ek kaagaz ka sliding darwaza narangi chamak raha hai, us par lady ki badi parchhai jhoom rahi hai.
```
Over-the-shoulder shot. The young man (red muffler) creeps down narrow wooden cellar stairs, holding the lantern low. At the far end of a cluttered basement, a sliding paper screen door glows orange from candlelight behind it. On the paper, the huge shadow of a tall woman with raised arms sways slowly.
```
Audio: chanting tez, dil ki dhadkan, seedhi ki charr.

### 8. Ritual · 6s
Screen poora band nahi hai. Daraar se: candles ka ghera, beech me purana taboot, lady normal form me, normal aankhen, haath uthaye. Peeche deewar par aadmiyon ki photos, aakhri keel khaali.
```
Point of view through a narrow gap in the paper screen. A small candlelit cellar room: a ring of burning candles and chalk circles on the floor around an old wooden coffin. The tall gaunt woman stands over the coffin in her normal form - dark auburn curly hair, normal dark eyes, cream and orange check cardigan, barefoot - arms raised, chanting. No weapon in her hands yet. Behind her the wall is covered with rows of old pinned photographs of different men; at the very end, one empty nail. Slow push-in.
```
Audio: chanting, jisme ek hi shabd saaf sunai deta hai: "...twelfth..."

### 9. Pakda Gaya · 5s
Farsh CHARRR karta hai. Chanting ruk jaati hai. Lady dheere se sar ghumaati hai, seedha use dekhti hai, aur talwar utha leti hai.
```
Same point of view through the gap in the paper screen. A loud floorboard creak. The old woman freezes mid-chant and the candles flicker. Her head turns slowly toward the camera, normal dark eyes fixed on the viewer. Without looking away, she lifts the old rusted crossguard sword from beside the coffin - a straight, broad, hand-painted rusted blade with a dark crossguard and short brown handle, not a curved katana.
```
Audio: CHARRR → sannata → talwar ki khanak.

### 10. Bhaago · 5s
Wo upar bhaagta hai, lalten jhool rahi hai. Deewar pe talwar wali lambi parchhai. Lady bhaagti nahi, bas lambe shaant kadam.
```
Fast handheld tracking shot, tilted angle. The young man (red muffler) sprints up the cellar stairs and down a dark hallway, the lantern swinging wildly and throwing shaky light. Behind him the huge shadow of the tall woman holding the sword stretches across the wall. She follows with slow, long, calm strides.
```
Audio: haanfti saans, tez kadam, farsh pe ghisatti talwar.

## Production notes
- Environment tone **normal aur grounded** rakho: ordinary leafy trees, light natural mist, real old-house locations. Nightmare forest, twisted dead trees, gore ya over-the-top horror avoid karo - horror sirf story aur performance se aaye.
- Har Miora video prompt me **man/player ka visible action** likho: woh kya karta hai, kaise move karta hai, kya react karta hai. Sirf camera/style likhna kaafi nahi.
- Aspect ratio 16:9 rakho (game landscape-only hai).
- Har shot 5s ya 10s me generate karo aur edit me trim karo. 3–4s wale shots bhi 5s me bana ke kaat do.
- Title aur koi bhi text edit me daalo. AI video text ki spelling bigaad deta hai.
- Agar tool audio bhi banata hai toh Audio/dialogue line prompt me jod do, warna edit me voice-over aur SFX.
- Export 720p H.264. 52 sec lagbhag 13 MB, APK halka rahega.
- Cutscene menu ke BAAD chalega: DAAYAN'S HOUSE menu → BEGIN → cutscene → gameplay. Title card dobara nahi dikhana.
