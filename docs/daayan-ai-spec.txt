
===== PAGE 1 =====
DAAYAN AI SYSTEM — COMPLETE
IMPLEMENTATION SPECIFICATION
1. Objective
Implement a highly believable, responsive, and scary enemy AI named Daayan for the 3D first-person
horror game.
Daayan should feel intelligent without feeling like a cheating AI. Her behavior must be driven by:
Player visibility
Player-generated sounds
Last known player position
Memory and confidence
Navigation/pathfinding
Search behavior
Controlled prediction
Context-aware decision making
Human-like movement and reactions
Do NOT use an LLM, neural network, or machine-learning model for the core gameplay AI. Use
deterministic game-AI systems that are efficient enough for real-time gameplay.
The AI must prioritize believability, suspense, fairness, and horror over raw optimality.
2. HIGH-LEVEL ARCHITECTURE
Create Daayan as a modular AI system:
DaayanAI ├── PerceptionSystem │ ├── VisionSystem │ └── HearingSystem │ ├──
MemorySystem │ ├── LastSeenPosition │ ├── LastHeardPosition │ ├── LastKnownPosition │
└── Confidence │ ├── DecisionSystem │ └── Hierarchical Finite State Machine │ ├──
NavigationSystem │ ├── Pathfinding │ ├── Waypoints │ └── Dynamic Destination Updates │
├── SearchSystem │ ├── Search Area Selection │ ├── Search Points │ └── Search Timeout │
├── PredictionSystem │ ├── MovementController │ ├── Acceleration │ ├── Deceleration │
├── Turn Speed │ └── Rotation Smoothing │ └── AnimationController
Keep each subsystem independent so it can be modified or tuned without rewriting the entire AI.

7. VISION CONFIDENCE
Do not make detection binary.
Maintain:
visionConfidence = 0.0 → 1.0
Increase confidence while the player is visible.
Decrease confidence while the player is partially visible or hidden.
Example concept:
Visible continuously: confidence rapidly increases.
Player briefly moves behind an object: confidence decreases.
Player fully disappears: confidence decays over time.
Use hysteresis so the AI does not rapidly switch between visible/not-visible every frame.

Possible sound types:
PLAYER_FOOTSTEP PLAYER_SPRINT DOOR_OPEN DOOR_CLOSE OBJECT_DROP OBJECT_THROW
OBJECT_BREAK FALL SPECIAL_NOISE

Do NOT make her instantly know that the player was responsible.
The sound only provides a probable location.

10. MEMORY SYSTEM
Daayan must remember recent player information.
Store:
lastSeenPosition lastSeenTime lastHeardPosition lastHeardTime lastKnownPosition confidence
currentTarget targetVelocity
When she sees the player:
lastSeenPosition = player.position
When she hears the player:
lastHeardPosition = sound.position
lastKnownPosition should represent the most reliable current estimate of where the player is.
Memory must decay over time.
The AI should gradually become less certain when the player has not been observed.
11. CHASE SYSTEM
When Daayan clearly sees the player:
transition into CHASE.
During chase:
Continuously update target position.
Continuously evaluate line of sight.
Recalculate navigation target periodically.
Maintain realistic movement acceleration.
Maintain realistic turn speed.
Avoid teleport-like movement.
Avoid instant 180-degree rotations.
Do NOT calculate expensive pathfinding every render frame.
Use throttled updates.

30. IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION PRINCIPLE
Do NOT try to make Daayan omniscient.
The AI should make decisions using:
WHAT SHE CAN SEE
+ WHAT SHE CAN HEAR
+ WHAT SHE REMEMBERS
+ WHAT SHE CAN REASONABLY PREDICT
Nothing more.
The player must be able to break line of sight, hide, create distractions, manipulate sound, and exploit the
house layout.
However, Daayan should be smart enough to investigate intelligently and sometimes anticipate likely player
movement.
The desired result is:
"Daayan feels intelligent."
NOT:
"Daayan knows everything."

The final Daayan AI should produce behavior where:

Sound investigation is meaningful.
